You are a subject in an online psychology experiment.
In this expriment, you are asked to read stories and answer questions about them according to you best judgement based on your intuitive world knowledge.
Specifically, you will read stories about two friends talking about prices of objects.
Please read the scenarios carefully. Your task is to answer a question, given the story, by rating the likelihood of the price of the item described to give your best estimate. Use the following scale: Impossible-1, Not very likely-2, Neutral-3, Very Likely-4, Extremely Likely-5.
Write your final answer as 'A:<rating>'.
Always answer with an option from 1-5.
